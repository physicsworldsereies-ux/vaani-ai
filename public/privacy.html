Information we collect

When you sign in with Google, Vaani receives account name, email address, profile image and account identifier. Vaani stores your profile, credit balance, chat history, credit usage and order/payment references to provide the service.

AI processing

Your prompts and relevant chat history are sent to Google Gemini to generate responses. Do not submit passwords, payment credentials or other sensitive information in chat.

Payments

Payments are processed by Razorpay. Vaani does not intentionally store full card numbers, UPI PINs or banking credentials. Payment status and identifiers may be stored for fulfilment and support.
