Pricing & Credit Packs

Effective date: 27 October 2026

Vaani offers optional digital credit packs for use with its AI tutoring service. Before you pay

  • Check the pack name, total price, and number of credits displayed on the checkout screen.

  • Do not complete payment if the displayed pack, price, or credit quantity is incorrect. Contact support first.
