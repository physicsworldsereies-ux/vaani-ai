Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026

This policy applies to Vaani's digital credit packs. Review it before completing payment.

1. Failed, pending or duplicate payments

If your account is not credited after a successful debit, or you believe you were charged more than once for the same order, contact support with the Razorpay order ID, payment ID, transaction date and amount. Do not send card numbers, CVV, UPI PINs, OTPs or passwords. We will check the payment status and, where a duplicate or failed transaction is confirmed, initiate the applicable refund through the payment provider.

2. Refund requests for credit packs

If purchased credits have not been used, you may request cancellation and a refund within 7 calendar days of purchase. We will review the order and usage record and respond within 5 business days. Credits already consumed are not refundable, except where required by applicable law or where the service error caused the charge. Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method; the bank or payment provider may take additional time to post the amount.

3. Service errors

If a technical error causes a confirmed incorrect charge or prevents delivery of purchased credits, contact support. We will investigate and, where appropriate, restore the credits or initiate a refund. We will explain the resolution to you.

4. How to request a refund

Email [REQUIRED — monitored support email] with subject “Vaani refund request” and include your account email, order ID, payment ID (if available), amount, date and a short description. Never include full payment credentials. We aim to acknowledge requests within 2 business days and provide a decision within 5 business days.

5. Refund processing

Approved refunds are initiated through the payment provider to the original payment method. After initiation, the time for funds to appear depends on the bank, card issuer, UPI app or payment provider. We will share the available refund reference when provided by the payment provider.
